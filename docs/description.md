[Revid](https://www.revid.ai) turns your posts into short videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and X: a script written from the post, a natural voiceover, captions, visuals and music.

- **From the editor.** Open a saved entry, choose **Make a Revid video** in its menu, and follow the render in the **Revid video** panel.
- **On publish.** Turn on automatic videos and every newly published post gets one.
- **For your AI agent.** Enable the plugin's MCP tools and the agent on your site can call `make_video` and `video_status`.

A published entry is summarized from its public page; a draft is sent as text. Videos render in your Revid account, where you can edit, export and schedule them.
